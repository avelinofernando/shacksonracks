Market research

Find the owners before the wrecking crew does.

I map who tears down houses in the valley, who has land waiting for one and what each of them needs to hear. Every ad, page and message after that is aimed with it.

Owners planning a teardownFound through demolition and building permits in Teton County and the Town of Jackson.
Builders and architectsThey are behind most remodels. One partner can send you several houses a year.
People with landLot owners in Star Valley, Teton Valley and Alpine looking for an affordable home.
Employers housing their staffBusinesses and housing groups looking for homes for local workers.

You get: a report with each group, how to reach it and the message that fits it.

Automated ads

Facebook, Instagram and Google ads for Teton County, Star Valley and Teton Valley. Simple rules move the budget to what brings conversations and pause what doesn't, and a short report lands in your inbox every month. We measure texts and calls, not likes.

Chatbot

Answers in seconds, at any hour.

In the US, most people reach a local business by text, so the assistant starts there, on a text-enabled business number. On iPhones it can appear in Messages as a verified business. The same assistant answers on your website, Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp, which helps with Spanish-speaking clients. It also signs up landowners and texts them when a house that fits is listed. When someone is ready, it hands them to you.
